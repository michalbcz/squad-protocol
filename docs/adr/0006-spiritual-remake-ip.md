# 0006. Spiritual remake: original name, art, maps and text

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Kind:** Game design
- **Affects:** packages/content, DESIGN.md §Scope

## Context

"Laser Squad" and its assets belong to their rights holders. We want to distribute on Steam and in the app stores.

## Decision

- The working title is **Squad Protocol**. Game mechanics are reimplemented from scratch, in the genre's tradition (AP, opportunity fire, destructible terrain, equipment phase).
- All names, scenarios, maps, art, audio and text are original. Content must not copy original scenario names or layouts.

## Consequences

Contributors and agents must not add original assets or scenario recreations. Inspired-by mechanics are fine.

## Alternatives considered

- **Licensed faithful remake** – needs rights we don't have.
- **Private fan project** – rules out store distribution.
